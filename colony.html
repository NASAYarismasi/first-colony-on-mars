      ℹ Scientific limitations and assumptions

      Legacy air model: 0.108 kg O₂/h, 2.7 kW. Continuous peak output from nine small MOXIE units is assumed; this plant has not been validated on Mars. Fan power of 0.3 kW and CO₂ removal power of 0.5 kW are preliminary design values. CO₂ mass is not a ppm reading or safety indicator.

      Water: 20 L per 6 hours at 2 kW; 50% ice and 80% capture. Recovery: 85%, 0.3 kW and 5 L/h. Human consumption is converted from Earth-day to hourly rates. Contaminants, excavation resistance and initial heating details are not modeled.

      Cabin pressure, temperature, humidity and gas composition are not calculated. Greenhouse airlock pressure animation is conceptual. No validated oxygen production credit is granted for algae or plants. These models do not guarantee real life-support adequacy.
